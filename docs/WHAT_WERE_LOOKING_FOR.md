# What We're Looking For

A big part of how we work at Marble is aligning up front on what great looks like. In that spirit, here's exactly what we'll be paying attention to in this session:

- **Ownership.** You're in the driver's seat - lead the scoping conversation, drive the build, and treat what we aligned on as a starting point, not a contract: if a better idea comes to you mid-build, act on it.

- **Product sense.** Thoughtful product decisions and clear trade-offs — being thoughtful about what matters, what doesn't, and being able to clearly explain your reasoning.

- **AI fluency.** Using AI to make yourself dramatically more effective — this is obviously a lot to build, so using AI to move quickly and then having good judgment about when to lean on it vs. think for yourself, and verifying what it produces.
