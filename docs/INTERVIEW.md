# Interview Format

The session is 2 hours in person.

---

## Agenda

| Phase | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Scoping** | ~20 min | You drive a product scoping session. |
| **Build** | ~60 min | You build, heads down. One check-in halfway through. |
| **Walkthrough** | ~20 min | You demo the product, talk through decisions made, and answer questions. |
| **Your questions for us** | ~10 min | Ask us anything about Marble, the team, culture, etc. |


---

## Scoping (20 min)

You'll drive a scoping conversation about the intake & matching product described you want to build. The *what* and the *why* are given — this conversation is for you to ask us questions & get input on the right way to build it.

Treat us as your braintrust — we're here to provide context, feedback on ideas, input on trade-offs, etc.

We should end with a shared high level understanding of what you're going to build.

---

## Build (60 min)

We'll leave the room. You build.

- **~30 min in**, one of us will pop back briefly for a check-in: how's it going, any questions or support needed.
- Otherwise: heads down, and if you need us we'll be right outside.
- If you finish the main product we scoped early, there's plenty of opportunity for extra credit, be creative!

---

## Walkthrough (20 min)

Come prepared to demo the running product end-to-end: walk us through intake flow & matching experience, the decisions & tradeoffs you made and why, etc.

We'll ask follow-up questions throughout. Expect us to probe on product judgment, technical implementation details, and where you'd go from here in a real world setting.

---

## Your questions for us (10 min)

We'll save 10 minutes at the end for you to ask us anything — about Marble, the team, the work, what we're investing in, what's hard right now. Come with questions.
